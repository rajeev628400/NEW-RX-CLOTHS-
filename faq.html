A few answers

FAQs

How should I read catalogue prices and availability?

Prices and stock are configured demonstration data for a learning project. Prices are not verified market or supplier prices, and stock is not reserved.

Can I place an order?

Not through the browser-saved bag. Cash on Delivery is the only payment method, but order placement is unavailable until the authenticated order service is connected and validates the order successfully.

How can I contact support?

Support details will be published when a customer support channel is available. Visit the contact page for the current status.
